This Cookie Policy explains how True Edge AB uses cookies and similar technologies on the Jameye landing site at jameye.com (the "Site"). Read it alongside the Privacy Policy.

## 1. Current position

As of the date above, the Site:

- sets **no first-party cookies**;
- sets **no third-party cookies**;
- uses **no** `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`;
- runs **no** analytics (no Google Analytics, GTM, or equivalent);
- runs **no** advertising or social pixels;
- embeds **no** third-party video or widgets that set cookies (video is self-hosted).

Because no non-essential cookies are set, a cookie consent banner is not currently required. This will change if the tools described in Section 5 are added.

## 2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and SDKs. They let a site function, remember choices, or measure usage.

## 4. Referral links (not a cookie)

The Site supports referral links of the form `jameye.com/?ref=<handle>`. The handle in the URL is read in your browser only, to pre-fill the "referred by" field in the waitlist form. It is not written to any cookie or storage. It is sent to our backend only if you complete the waitlist signup. See the Privacy Policy for how that data is handled.

## 5. Future changes

The team intends to add analytics (Google Analytics 4), advertising tags (Google Ads, and X, TikTok, and Reddit pixels), and Google OAuth login. When any of these ships, the Site will set analytics and marketing cookies, and EU law (GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive) will require a consent banner that obtains your prior opt-in before any non-essential cookie is set. When that happens, this policy will be replaced with the expanded version listing every cookie, its provider, category, and duration, and the consent controls will go live at the same time.

## 6. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Because the Site currently sets no non-essential cookies, no action is needed to avoid tracking today.
